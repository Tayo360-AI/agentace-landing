    Why 90 days? Why not lifetime access?

    Because 90 days is the real study cycle for AI-201. Nobody preps for this exam for 6 months — the material is too fast-moving. AgentAce is priced for the window you'll actually use it. If you need more time, you can extend for $29 (60 more days) or re-purchase.

    What happens to my data after 90 days?

    Your profile, streak, quiz history, and progress stay in your account. You just lose access to the AI Tutor, Coach, and drill generation. Log back in anytime by extending or re-purchasing — nothing is deleted.

    Do you offer a refund if I don't pass?

    We do better than a refund. If you fail the exam after completing at least 70% of drill questions and one full mock exam, we extend your access by another 90 days — free. That's more valuable than $49 back, because it gives you time to actually pass.

    Is this affiliated with Salesforce?

    No. AgentAce is an independent third-party study tool. We're not endorsed by Salesforce, and we don't have access to real exam questions. Every question in AgentAce is originally authored based on the publicly documented 2026 blueprint.

    How is this different from Focus on Force or a Udemy course?

    Focus on Force gives you a static question bank. Udemy gives you video lectures. Both are one-size-fits-all. AgentAce is a personalized AI tutor and coach that adapts to what you already know and what you're missing. Think of it as the difference between reading a textbook and having a study partner who knows your weak spots.

    Will it stay current when Salesforce ships new releases?

    Yes. AgentAce updates every Salesforce release (Spring, Summer, Winter — three per year). When new features hit — like the recent Data Cloud → Data 360 rename or the Flex Apex capability retirement — the content updates automatically. You'll see a banner when new content is live.

    Can I use it on mobile?

    Yes. AgentAce works on any modern browser — desktop, tablet, or phone. The interface adapts. Your progress syncs across devices when you're signed in.
